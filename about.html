About Afterglow

Vintage clothing, furniture, and one-of-a-kind finds in Denver, Colorado.

Vintage with character.

Afterglow Vintage is a locally owned shop in Denver featuring a carefully selected mix of vintage clothing, denim, footwear, furniture, and home goods.

Co-owners Heather and Alejandro share a love for pieces with history. Heather is drawn to vintage clothing, art, books, and music, while Alejandro has a passion for furniture, artwork, and housewares.

At Afterglow, vintage is about more than style. It is about appreciating the quality and stories behind older pieces and giving them a new place to belong.

Whether you are searching for a classic pair of jeans or something special for your home, the shop offers plenty to discover.
